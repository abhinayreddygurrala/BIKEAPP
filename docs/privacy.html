Odomap

Privacy Policy

Effective September 28, 2026

The short version: Odomap does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any of your data. There are no accounts, no sign-in, and no company servers. Everything you record stays on your phone.

What we collect

Nothing. Odomap has no backend. There is no server for your information to travel to, so there is nothing for us to collect, store on our end, or share with anyone — including advertisers, analytics providers, or any other third party. The app contains no analytics or tracking software of any kind.

What's stored, and where

Everything you enter into Odomap — bikes, rides, maintenance records, fuel logs, and any photos you attach — is stored in a database and file storage located only on your device. None of it is uploaded, synced, or backed up anywhere by us.

Permissions Odomap asks for

Location To record your ride's route, distance, and speed — including briefly in the background while a ride is actively being recorded and your phone is locked.
Motion & Fitness To measure your bike's lean angle while a ride is being recorded.
Camera & Photo Library To attach a profile picture, a photo of your bike, or a photo of a maintenance receipt.

Data captured through any of these permissions is saved only in your device's local storage, exactly like the rest of your Odomap data.

On-device AI

Odomap's trip-planning feature uses Apple Intelligence, when it's available on your phone, to answer questions about scenic routes. This runs entirely on your device using Apple's own on-device model — your questions are not sent to us or to any server, because none exists for this app.

Exporting your data

Odomap lets you export all of your data to a single file from Settings. This uses your phone's built-in Share Sheet, which only acts when you choose where to send the file — nothing is uploaded automatically.

Deleting your data

You can delete individual bikes, rides, or records at any time from within the app. Uninstalling Odomap permanently deletes everything, since nothing exists outside your device.

Children's privacy

Odomap does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect information from anyone at all.

Changes to this policy

If Odomap's data practices ever change, this page will be updated before that change takes effect.
