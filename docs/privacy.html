Odomap

Privacy Policy

Effective October 2, 2026

The short version: Odomap needs a free account. What you record is kept on your phone and saved to your account in our cloud, photos included, so you can get it back on a new phone. We never sell your data, never use it for advertising, and the app has no ads, analytics, or tracking.

Odomap is made by an independent developer (“we” and “us” in this policy). This policy explains what information the app handles, where it goes, and the choices you have.

What we collect

To use Odomap you create a free account. We then collect and store:

Account details Your username, email address, phone number, and password. Your password is stored only in a scrambled one-way form (a hash), so nobody, including us, can read it.
Your Odomap data Your bikes, finished rides (including each ride's GPS route, speed, and lean-angle stats), maintenance records, fuel logs, expenses, profile name and bio, and app settings such as units and text size. A ride is saved to your account only once you finish it.
Photos and documents Your profile picture, bike photos, and the receipt photos and PDFs you attach to records.
Technical information When the app talks to our server, the server sees your IP address and records basic details of each request (such as the time and which feature was used). We use this only to keep the service secure, for example to block repeated wrong-password attempts, and to fix problems.

Saving is automatic: a few seconds after you add, change, or delete something, the change is saved to your account. When you sign out, your data is removed from the phone and stays in your account; signing in again brings it back.

How we use your information

We use it only to run your account and to save and restore your data. Your email address and phone number identify your account and are never used for marketing. We do not sell your information, share it with advertisers or data brokers, or use it to build a profile of you. No other Odomap user can see your account details or your data.

Where it's stored and how it's protected

Your account and backed-up data are stored on servers we rent from Google Cloud in the United States. Google only hosts the data for us; it doesn't use it for its own purposes.

Everything travels between the app and our servers over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection, and Google Cloud encrypts the stored data. Your photos sit in private storage that can only be reached through short-lived links issued to your own account. On your phone, your sign-in is kept in the iPhone's secure Keychain.

Services Odomap uses

Google Maps Maps in Odomap are provided by Google Maps. When a map is on screen, Google receives the area you're viewing, your IP address, and, if the map is showing where you are, your location. Google handles this under its own Privacy Policy, and your use of the maps is subject to the Google Maps Additional Terms of Service.
Place search and route planning When you search for a place or plan a route in the Navigation tab, what you type and the start and end points of the route are sent to our server, which asks Google (Google Places and Google Routes) to find the places and routes. These requests aren't linked to your account, and our server doesn't keep what you searched for or where you were going; it only counts how many requests it makes each month.
Apple Maps If Google isn't available, Odomap shows Apple's map instead and asks Apple to find places and routes; those requests go from your phone straight to Apple, which handles them under its own privacy policy.
Turn-by-turn directions When you tap to ride a planned route, the route's start, end, and a few points along it are opened in the Google Maps app (or Apple Maps), which handles them under its own privacy policy.
Apple Intelligence Receipt scanning uses Apple's on-device AI when your phone supports it. It runs entirely on your phone, and your receipt photos aren't sent to us or to anyone else.

Permissions Odomap asks for

Location To record your ride's route, distance, and speed, including in the background while a ride is being recorded and your phone is locked, and to show where you are on the map.
Motion & Fitness To measure your bike's lean angle while a ride is being recorded.
Camera & Photo Library To add a profile picture, a photo of your bike, or a photo of a receipt.
Notifications To remind you when maintenance is due. Reminders are scheduled on your phone; we don't send them from a server.

What these permissions capture is stored on your phone. If you have an account, your finished rides and photos are also saved to it, as described above.

Deleting your data

Single items: when you delete a bike, ride, record, or photo in the app, it's also deleted from your account the next time the app saves. Deleting a bike also deletes its service records, expenses, and fuel logs; your rides are kept.

Your whole account: go to Settings → Delete Account. This permanently deletes your account details and everything saved to it, including your photos and documents, from our servers, and removes your data from the phone.

Our storage provider keeps deleted photos and documents hidden for 7 days before erasing them for good, so a mistake can be undone. Our server also makes an automatic safety copy every night; each copy is deleted after 7 days and erased for good 7 days after that. Deleted information is fully gone within 14 days.

Your choices

You can change your password in Settings, sign out (which removes your data from the phone but keeps it in your account), and delete your account at any time. To get a copy of the information we hold about you, or to change your email or phone number, email us at the address below and we'll respond within 30 days.

Children's privacy

Odomap is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Odomap handles your information, we'll update this page and its date before the change takes effect. If we add features that share information with other riders, such as group chat, this policy will be updated before those features launch.
