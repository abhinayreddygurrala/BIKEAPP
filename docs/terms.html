Riding a motorcycle is dangerous. Odomap records and reviews your rides; it is not a safety device and doesn't make riding safer.

  • Never look at, tap, or set up the app while riding. Start a ride before you set off, and stop it once you've parked safely.
  • If you mount your phone where you can see it, mount it securely, and follow the laws where you ride on phone use, helmets, and equipment.
  • Speed, distance, lean angle, G-force, acceleration times, and other figures are estimates from your phone's GPS and motion sensors. They can be wrong.
