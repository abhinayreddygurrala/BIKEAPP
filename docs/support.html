Common questions

Do I need to create an account?

No. Tap “Skip for now” and everything works without one. A free account saves your data to the cloud automatically, so it's safe if you lose or switch phones.

Where is my data stored?

On your phone. If you create an account, every change also saves to your Odomap account in the cloud — photos and receipts included — so you can bring it all back on a new phone. Without an account, your data stays only on your phone.

How do I back up my rides, bikes, and maintenance history?

Create a free account in Settings → Sign In or Create Account. From then on, everything saves automatically a few seconds after each change — there's nothing to press. On a new phone, sign in and choose Restore.

Why does Odomap ask for location access in the background?

Only while you're actively recording a ride, so your route keeps recording if your phone locks or you switch apps mid-ride.

The AI trip planner isn't available — why?

It requires Apple Intelligence to be turned on for your phone (Settings → Apple Intelligence & Siri), and a phone that supports it.
